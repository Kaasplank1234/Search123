🔒 Privacy & Security

Search123 is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, track, or sell any personal data. Your searches are not stored, your IP address is not logged, and no cookies are set.

Every search query is routed through our secure infrastructure and processed without any form of profiling or targeted advertising.

For complete details about our privacy practices, security measures, and data handling, please visit the GitHub repository documentation.

ℹ️ About Search123

Search123 is a modern metasearch engine developed by Cheesy Studios. It aggregates results from multiple search engines while maintaining complete user privacy.

The project is entirely open-source, meaning anyone can review the code, report security issues, and contribute improvements. We believe transparency and community involvement are essential for building trustworthy tools.

Whether you're concerned about search privacy, want a faster search experience, or simply believe in open-source software, Search123 is designed for you.
